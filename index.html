    Questions, answered

    Will ApplyMate write my essays for me?

    No. It helps you find topics, structure your story and tighten your draft, but the writing is yours. Admissions readers can tell, and so can you.

    Who is it for?

    Students applying to undergraduate or graduate programmes, and the parents helping them, who want structure and expert-level guidance at a fair price.

    How is it more affordable than a consultant?

    AI handles the repetitive work such as shortlisting, tracking and form filling. Human experts spend their time on review, where they add the most value.

    Is my data safe?

    Your profile and documents are used only to build your application journey, and you can delete them at any time.
